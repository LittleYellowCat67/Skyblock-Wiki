# Tree Gifts
Aliases: Fig Tree Gift, Fig Tree Gifts, Helix Tree Gift, Helix Tree Gifts, Mangrove Tree Gift, Mangrove Tree Gifts, Tree Gift
[Tree Gifts]
Tree Gifts are bundles of loot that is rewarded after a tree is fully cut down on [[Galatea]]. [[Fig Log|Fig]], [[Mangrove Log|Mangrove]], and [[Helix Log|Helix]] trees each have separate loot pools for Tree Gifts.
## Obtaining
A Tree Gift is awarded after fully cutting a tree on [[Galatea]]. The quantity of guaranteed rewards and chance for rare rewards scales based on:
- the amount a player contributes to chopping down a tree;
- the tree's size multiplier, ranging from 1x to 20x.
|Percent Cut|Rewards|
|-|-|
|<10%|No rewards|
|10%|Normal rewards|
|20%|50% more rewards|
|33%|Double rewards|

# Trial of Blue Flames
Aliases: Soul Campfire, Soul campfire
The Trial of Blue Flames is a challenge found in the [[South Reaches]] of [[Galatea]]. The player must stand inside a soul campfire for 10 seconds of increasing damage to earn and improve an [[Accessories|Accessory]]. The Trial of Blue Flames can be started by talking to [[Brian]]. If the DPS is higher than the player's max [[Health]], the player instantly fails.
## Trials

# Wormhole
Aliases: Wormholes
[Wormhole]
Wormholes are temporary rings on the [[Lotus Atoll]] and [[Crimson Isle]] that appear in various locations above water or lava. They can only be seen while wearing [[Froggles]], [[Silver Froggles]], [[Golden Froggles]], or [[Diamond Froggles]].
## Location
Wormholes spawn on the [[Lotus Atoll]] and [[Crimson Isle]].

## Usage
Fishing inside a Wormhole allows the player to catch [[Trophy Frogs|Reality Hopper]] [[Trophy Frogs]] while on the [[Lotus Atoll]] and also prevents [[Sea Creatures]] from being caught. Only 2 Wormholes can exist on the [[Lotus Atoll]] at once. After 4-6 catches, a Wormhole vanishes and reappears somewhere else with the following message:
Your Wormhole closed up...
Additionally, some items gain bonuses from fishing inside a Wormhole.
